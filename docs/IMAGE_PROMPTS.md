Generate the cut-outs below when image generation is available, then review each file for anatomy, perspective, edge quality, lighting, and alpha before marking it final.

All image prompts share this art direction: editorial product photography for a thoughtful Indonesian creative technology studio; realistic Indonesian home proportions; soft daylight from a window; warm white, timber, and subtle blue accents; restrained styling; natural contact shadows; clean composition; no text, typography, logos, watermarks, UI, borders, grids, or duplicated objects. Avoid beige/terracotta casts, exaggerated wide-angle perspective, glossy plastic, floating objects, extra or missing furniture parts, and mirrored duplicates. Use a three-quarter camera view unless the role prompt says otherwise.
